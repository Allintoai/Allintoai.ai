Before you start

Can I explore without an account?

You can open the workspace and review its controls. Saving and reopening private records requires sign-in. The ROI calculator is available without signing in.

Does WorPrex execute the workflow?

No. The current beta proposes a pipeline for review. It does not run external tools, trigger jobs or deploy integrations.

Where is my workflow saved?

Supported cloud saves are associated with your account. If cloud saving is unavailable, the application labels a save as device-only. Device-only records are not a cross-device backup and may be lost when browser data is cleared.

Does WorPrex cost extra?

WorPrex is included in the current ModRecx offering. Review Plans & pricing for the applicable plan and recommendation limits.
